# Hi there, I'm Daniel Tashobya 👋😄

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at Strathmore University with a strong pull towards **data engineering**. I enjoy building the plumbing that turns raw, messy data into something reliable and useful: ETL pipelines, data warehouses, dashboards and the predictive models that sit on top of them. Most of my work is aimed at real problems close to home, from dairy farm records to research and health data.

-  **Current Focus:** My final-year project, ETL pipelines for agricultural (cattle farming) data, alongside [MilkYield_Recommender_System](https://github.com/Danieltash0/MilkYield_Recommender_System), a dairy farm management platform that predicts future milk yields.
-  **Learning & Exploration:** Data warehousing and visualisation for analytics and predictive modelling, applied machine learning, and embedded systems / IoT.
-  **Core Interests:** Data Engineering, Machine Learning, Data Visualisation.
-  **Experience:** Data engineering internship at ACE-Uganda (African Center of Excellence in Bioinformatics & Data Sciences, Makerere University Infectious Diseases Institute), working on ETL pipelines, MySQL data warehousing, Docker and Metabase dashboards.
